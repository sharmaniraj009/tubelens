A weekly plan that works for most channels

  1. One long video a week, at the same time. Use your best slot above, or the time your audience is online in YouTube Studio's Analytics. Two a week only if you can keep it up.
  2. Two or three Shorts a week from that video. Save its most replayed moments in the Clips tab and plan them here. Post them on the days in between, and set the full video as each Short's Related video so viewers can follow it.
  3. A community post or poll mid-week. Ask what viewers want next; the answers are your next titles.
  4. The first hours after publishing. Pin a comment that asks a question and answer early comments, questions first. The Engage tab in the video panel drafts the comment and finds who to answer.
  5. After 24–48 hours, compare. Check the video's views per hour against your usual. If it lags, test a new thumbnail or title before changing anything else.

Common practice, not a promise. Check it against your own numbers above and keep what works.
